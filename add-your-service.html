Add or update your team's listing

New listings are added through the RSSN Research IT Provider Questionnaire. Existing listings are updated by request, by email.

Add a new listing

Complete the RSSN Research IT Provider Questionnaire. Your answers become your team's listing, and RSSN reviews them before it goes live.

Start the questionnaire

Already filled out the questionnaire?

You don't need to do anything else. RSSN imports questionnaire responses into listings and will ask you to review yours before it goes live. After that, please request changes by email, as described below, rather than resubmitting the questionnaire.

Update an existing listing

The easy way: open your team's profile and click See all answers & request a change via email, or choose your team here. You'll see every question with all of its answer choices and your team's current answers selected. Change what's out of date, and the page writes an email to RSSN listing your changes. RSSN staff make the changes in the questionnaire, and the listing is updated from there.
