About the RSSN Provider Inventory

The Research Software Support Network (RSSN) connects Johns Hopkins researchers with the teams across the university and health system that build, run, and support research software, data systems, and computing.

Each listing comes from the RSSN Research IT Provider Questionnaire and is maintained by the team it describes. Use the search and filters on the Find support page to narrow the list by service, institution, school, and who each team can work with.

How listings stay current

This site is built from files in a public GitHub repository, one file per team. Teams update their own listing by proposing a change on GitHub, and an RSSN reviewer approves it before it appears here. Every change is recorded, so it's always clear who changed what and when.

Want your team included? See Add your service.
