Request a change to a listing

This page shows everything a team's listing says, as the questionnaire asked it: every question, every answer choice, and the team's current answers already selected. Change anything that's out of date, then use Prepare my email at the bottom. The page writes a short summary of your changes for you to email to RSSN, and RSSN updates the listing. Nothing is saved or sent until you send the email yourself.

Comfortable with GitHub? You can also edit the listing file directly.

Anything else

Your name and email only go into the email you send, so RSSN can follow up. They aren't saved or shown on the site.
