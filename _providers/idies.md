We provide support to scientific collaborations who wish to share and analyze their, possibly very large data sets. We specialize in storing files and relational databases and provide targeted compute environments through the SciServer platform for accessing, analyzing and disseminating these. We have personnel supporting the whole hierarchy from data center sysadmins up to ML experts.
